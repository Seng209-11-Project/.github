# Private Repos that can only be shared on request:

## dorm-notif, dorm-notif-admin
A mobile app that was a contract gig aiming to digitilize the workflow of a dorm ecosystem, providing a better channel for announcements, leave-requests, complaints
payment auditing while being integrated to a push notification service while also providing the students QOL features such as the ability to view which room they are in while providing 
them with contact info of both their roommates and dorm personnel.

## tmgdk-app, tmgdk-app-eu 
A web app that was a contract gig aiming to digitilize the workflow of TMGDK's (Tehlikeli Madde Güvenliği Danışma Kurumu) allowing them to 
provide easy acess to client information / files while providing the ability to overview employees permissions / interactions with the
clients while also providing auto filling of regulatory documents  using client information and providing e-signatures. 

## aig-sight
A website that is a gig aiming to create a nice modern website for an optician brand while also integrating shopping, while providing a link between other 3rd party sites
such as trendyol automatically updating stocks and pricing from one panel.
